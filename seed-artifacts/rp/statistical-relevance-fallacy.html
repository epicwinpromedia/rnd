The Statistical Relevance Fallacy

Why Probabilistic Confidence Cannot Substitute for Structural Verification

Bryan Lee Harris · EPIC WIN Pro Media · 2026-10-18

Markdown (.md) PDF (.pdf) JSON-LD (.jsonld) All Papers

Abstract

Vector similarity and next-token probability measure linguistic proximity, not factual truth or legal compliance. In enterprise customer storytelling and regulated developer platforms, compositive AI pipelines must gate probabilistic outputs behind deterministic thresholds and human-approved source receipts.

KEY RECEIPT

Calibrated Companion Noul thresholds (<0.3 out-of-scope, 0.3-0.7 top-2 suggestions, >0.7 verified receipt) enforce zero hallucinated prose in production.

1. Proximity Is Not Proof

A retrieval system that returns a 0.89 cosine-similarity chunk can still fabricate a customer metric or misquote a CISO. Statistical relevance tells you two paragraphs share vocabulary; it never guarantees that the resulting claim will survive Legal, Comms, or engineering review.

2. Compositive AI vs. Generative Slop

The Comprehensive Brand Profile (CBP) architecture treats approved primary-source interviews as immutable receipts. AI is used to route, structure, and atomize verified facts across channels (keynote, dev blog, CISO brief, social video) under explicit brand guardrails rather than inventing copy from thin air.

3. Auditable Decision Traces

Every automated routing decision should expose its confidence score, matched intent, latency, and model identifier. Transparency turns AI from a black-box liability into an inspectable engineering instrument.
